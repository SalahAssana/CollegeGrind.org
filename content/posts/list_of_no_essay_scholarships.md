No‑essay and lottery‑style scholarships offer a fast, low‑barrier way to try for extra funding, especially when you’re already juggling classes, work, and traditional applications. These awards don’t measure GPA, essays, or extracurriculars; they’re simple drawings or quick‑entry forms anyone can submit. This guide brings together a clear, current list of active opportunities, along with what students should know about eligibility, odds, and how to use these scholarships responsibly as part of a broader financial aid strategy.

### A Word of Caution

There are [several drawbacks to no-essay scholarships](https://collegegrind.org/posts/no_essay_scholarships_explained) which should be considered before applying. Even though these quick‑apply scholarships can be worth a try, it’s important to remember the drawbacks outlined in the earlier guide. Most no‑essay and lottery‑style awards function as sweepstakes, which means extremely low odds of winning and a high likelihood that your personal information will be used for marketing rather than meaningful financial aid. To protect your privacy while still participating, it’s wise to create a separate email address and a dedicated phone number—such as a Google Voice number—so scholarship entries don’t flood your primary inbox or expose your main contact information to third‑party advertisers.

## Category 1: Monthly No‑Essay Scholarships

### Niche $2,000 “No Essay” Scholarship
- **Sponsoring organization / platform:** [Niche.com](https://www.niche.com/about/scholarship-rules/no-essay-scholarship/).
- **Award amount:** 2,000 dollars per sweepstakes period.
- **Frequency:** Monthly sweepstakes periods throughout 2026, with new entry windows such as March 1–31, 2026.
- **Eligibility criteria:** Open to [high school, college, and graduate students](https://www.niche.com/about/scholarship-rules/no-essay-scholarship/), and those planning to enroll within 12 months; must attend or plan to attend a school listed on Niche; U.S. citizens or those with valid U.S. visa/passport only.
- **Application requirements:** Short online entry form on Niche; no essay or recommendations; survey responses must be complete and accurate.
- **Application frequency:** One entry per person per monthly sweepstakes period.
- **Entry method:** Online form via the [official Niche scholarship rules](https://www.niche.com/about/scholarship-rules/no-essay-scholarship/) and application page.
- **Key notes:** Winner chosen by random drawing each period; entries are valid only for the period submitted and do not roll over.

### Sallie Mae $2,000 No Essay Scholarship (Monthly Sweepstakes)
- **Sponsoring organization / platform:** [Sallie (Sallie Mae)](https://www.sallie.com/scholarships/no-essay) / UsSweeps administration partner.
- **Award amount:** 2,000 dollars to one winner each month.
- **Frequency:** Monthly drawings with 2026 deadlines listed for every month from April through December.
- **Eligibility criteria:** Legal residents of the 50 U.S. states, District of Columbia, and U.S. territories; age 16 or older; must meet [Sallie scholarship rules](https://www.sallie.com/scholarships/no-essay/student-referral) (students or parents linked to high school or college enrollment).
- **Application requirements:** Short online form with a few questions; no essay; [no account profile required](https://www.fastweb.com/college-scholarships/scholarships/193241-2-000-no-essay-scholarship-by-sallie) according to Fastweb’s description.
- **Application frequency:** One entry per person per monthly sweepstakes period.
- **Entry method:** Online application on [Sallie’s official "No Essay Scholarship" page](https://www.sallie.com/scholarships/no-essay) or linked partners like [Fastweb](https://www.fastweb.com/college-scholarships/scholarships/193241-2-000-no-essay-scholarship-by-sallie) and [Access Scholarships](https://accessscholarships.com/scholarship/nitro-college-scholarship-sweepstakes/).
- **Key notes:** Funds are [paid directly to the winner’s school](https://www.sallie.com/scholarships/no-essay/student-referral) once enrollment is confirmed; non‑enrolled winners may have funds held until they enroll.

### ScholarshipPoints $2,500 Monthly Scholarship
- **Sponsoring organization / platform:** [ScholarshipPoints](https://www.scholarshippoints.com) (an Edvisors program).

- **Application frequency:** Multiple drawings each month; members can redeem points into each sweepstakes drawing according to program rules.
- **Entry method:** Online via ScholarshipPoints.com, with entry tied to membership and point redemptions.
- **Key notes:** Over 1,000,000 dollars in scholarships have been awarded; quick entry and [no essay](https://www.scholarshippoints.com), but engagement activities can boost odds.

### BigFuture Scholarships (500 and 40,000 Dollars for College Planning)

- **Award amounts:** Monthly 500‑dollar scholarships plus periodic [40,000‑dollar scholarships](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/pay-for-college/bigfuture-scholarships-2027) awarded by random drawing.

- **Entry method:** Online through BigFuture’s scholarship dashboard integrated with College Board accounts.
- **Key notes:** College Board notes that [lower‑income students can receive extra entries](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/pay-for-college/bigfuture-scholarships/family-income-criteria); since 2019, more than 20 million dollars in BigFuture Scholarships have been awarded.
