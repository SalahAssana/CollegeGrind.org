This reference guide compiles a wide range of niche and identity-based scholarships across the United States into a single structured resource, making it easier to discover targeted opportunities that match specific backgrounds, interests, and life circumstances. It organizes scholarships by demographic factors, academic fields, affiliations, personal interests, and unique life situations so students can quickly scan for programs aligned with their own profiles.

## Demographic-Based Scholarships

### The Gates Scholarship (TGS)
- **Sponsoring Organization:** Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation via [The Gates Scholarship program](https://www.thegatesscholarship.org/scholarship).
- **Award Amount:** [Last-dollar scholarship](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/scholarships/the-gates-scholarship) covering remaining cost of attendance (tuition, fees, room, board, books, and other costs) after other aid; award amount varies by student.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** [U.S. high school seniors](https://www.thegatesscholarship.org/scholarship), Pell-eligible, from low-income households, U.S. citizens or permanent residents, minimum weighted GPA of 3.3, planning full-time bachelor’s study at a U.S. not-for-profit college or university.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Demographic (historically underrepresented, low-income students), Circumstance-based (Pell-eligible, low-income).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** Multi-phase [online application](https://www.collegeadvisor.com/merit-based-scholarships/bill-gates-scholarship/) including academic information, activities, recommendations, financial aid documents, and participation in semifinalist and finalist rounds with interviews.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Highly competitive](https://www.collegeadvisor.com/merit-based-scholarships/bill-gates-scholarship/) (national program selecting a few hundred scholars annually from a very large applicant pool).
- **Key Notes:** Renewable up to completion of bachelor’s degree; designed as a [comprehensive support and leadership-development program](https://www.thegatesscholarship.org/about-gates) in addition to funding.

### Ron Brown Signature Scholarship (Ron Brown Scholar Program)
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [Ron Brown Scholar Program](https://ronbrown.org/ron-brown-scholarship/).
- **Award Amount:** Four-year renewable scholarship totaling 40,000 United States dollars (10,000 United States dollars per year); some [recent cohorts](https://www.accessandequity.org/ron-brown-scholarships/) note 45–50 awards annually.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** [Black/African American U.S. citizens](https://www.siths.org/apps/news/article/1360711) or permanent residents who are high school seniors, academically strong, demonstrate leadership, community service, and financial need.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Demographic (race/ethnicity: Black/African American).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** Application form, essays, recommendations, transcript, standardized test information, and documentation of activities; [finalists attend an in-person selection weekend](https://www.accessandequity.org/ron-brown-scholarships/) with interviews.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Highly competitive national award](https://www.accessandequity.org/ron-brown-scholarships/) with only dozens of scholars chosen each year.
- **Key Notes:** Scholars may attend [any accredited four-year U.S. college](https://nursing.nyu.edu/current-students/scholarships/ron-brown-scholars-program); funds are renewable and can be used for any field of study.

### Hispanic Scholarship Fund (HSF Scholarship)
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [Hispanic Scholarship Fund](https://fellowshipsearch.baruch.cuny.edu/fellowship/hispanic-scholarship-fund/).
- **Award Amount:** Competitive scholarships typically ranging from [500 to 5,000 United States dollars](https://accessscholarships.com/scholarship/hispanic-scholarship-fund-scholarship/) per year; HSF reports awarding more than 30 million United States dollars annually.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Students of [Hispanic heritage](https://www.scholarships.com/scholarships/hsf-scholarship) (U.S. citizens, permanent residents, DACA, or eligible non-citizens) who meet minimum GPA thresholds, demonstrate financial need, and plan full-time enrollment in degree programs.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Demographic (ethnicity/heritage: Hispanic/Latino).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Online application](https://accessscholarships.com/scholarship/hispanic-scholarship-fund-scholarship/), FAFSA or state aid forms where applicable, official transcript, and financial information; applicants may then be considered for multiple internal HSF awards.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** Highly competitive national pool but with thousands of awards annually.
- **Key Notes:** Open to high school, undergraduate, and graduate students in all majors, with emphasis on STEM; [HSF Scholars](https://www.buffalo.edu/fellowships/funding/hsf-scholar.html) gain access to mentoring, career programming, and summits in STEM, finance, media, entrepreneurship, and healthcare.

### Point Foundation LGBTQ+ Scholarships
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [Point Foundation – The National LGBTQ Scholarship Fund](https://pointfoundation.org/scholarships).
- **Award Amount:** Multi-year, need-based scholarships; individual award amounts vary, with funding structured as [“last provider” support](https://urf.columbia.edu/fellowship/point-foundation-scholarships) to close financial gaps.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Students who [openly identify as LGBTQ](https://pointfoundation.org/scholarships), enrolled or planning to enroll full time in accredited U.S. undergraduate or graduate degree programs, with strong academic records, leadership, and a demonstrated commitment to LGBTQ communities.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Demographic (sexual orientation and gender identity: LGBTQ+).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Detailed online application](https://onsa.asu.edu/scholarship/point-foundation-scholarship) including academic history, financial information, resume, personal statements about leadership and LGBTQ community work, recommendations, and often an interview.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** Highly competitive national program with a limited number of multi-year awards.
- **Key Notes:** Scholars receive [mentoring, leadership development, and community-building programming](https://pointfoundation.org/scholarships); some awards are structured as last-dollar scholarships to complement other aid.

### Society of Women Engineers (SWE) Scholarships
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [Society of Women Engineers](https://swe.org/scholarships/).
- **Award Amount:** More than 330 scholarships annually with total disbursements of around 1.5–1.6 million United States dollars per year; individual awards commonly range from [1,000 to 19,000 United States dollars](https://www.scholarships.com/scholarships/society-of-women-engineers-swe-scholarship-program).
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Students who [identify as women](https://accessscholarships.com/scholarship/society-of-women-engineers-freshman-scholarships/) pursuing ABET-accredited bachelor’s or graduate programs in engineering, engineering technology, computing, or closely related fields; many awards limited to U.S. citizens or permanent residents; minimum GPA requirements (often around 3.0).
- **Type of Scholarship:** Demographic (gender: women), Field-of-study (engineering, technology, computing).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Single online application](https://apply.mykaleidoscope.com/program/SWECollegiateApp26) used to match applicants to multiple SWE-administered awards, including academic records, program information, activities, essays, and at least one recommendation.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** Moderately to highly competitive given national reach but relatively large number of awards.
- **Key Notes:** Open to both SWE members and non-members for many awards; includes [separate application tracks](https://swe.org/scholarships/) for incoming first-year students and for sophomores through graduate students.

### American Indian College Fund Scholarships
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [American Indian College Fund](https://collegefund.org/students/scholarships/).
- **Award Amount:** [Hundreds of scholarship opportunities](https://collegefund.org/search/scholarship/) annually, with the organization reporting over 21 million United States dollars in funding supporting more than 7,300 scholars in a recent year; individual award amounts vary.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** [Enrolled members or descendants](https://collegefund.org/search/scholarships/) of enrolled members of state or federally recognized American Indian or Alaska Native tribes, with at least a 2.0 GPA, planning full-time enrollment in certificate, undergraduate, or graduate programs at tribal colleges and universities or other accredited non-profit institutions.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Demographic (tribal affiliation: American Indian and Alaska Native).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Single online application](https://collegefund.org/students/scholarships/) used to match applicants to multiple programs; requires proof of tribal enrollment or descent, academic transcripts, and enrollment plans.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** Competitive but with substantial volume of awards across many programs.
- **Key Notes:** Offers both college-specific and [national “Full Circle” scholarships](https://collegefund.org/search/scholarships/); application season typically opens February 1 and remains open until funds are allocated, with priority for early applicants.

### National Federation of the Blind Scholarship Program
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [National Federation of the Blind (NFB)](https://nfb.org/programs-services/scholarships-and-awards/scholarship-program).
- **Award Amount:** [Around thirty national-level merit scholarships](http://nabslink.org/scholarships) annually, commonly 8,000 United States dollars each, totaling more than 250,000 United States dollars in cash and prizes per year.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** [Legally blind in both eyes](https://www.accesslex.org/scholarship/national-federation-blind-scholarship-program), at least 18 years old, residing in the United States, District of Columbia, or Puerto Rico, and pursuing or planning full-time postsecondary degree study.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Demographic (disability: blindness/visual impairment).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Online application](https://scholarships.nfb.org), confirmation of legal blindness, transcripts, recommendations, personal statements, and an interview; finalists must attend the NFB national convention to remain eligible.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Highly competitive national program](https://nfb.org/programs-services/scholarships-and-awards/scholarship-program) with only thirty scholarships.
- **Key Notes:** Awards are based on academic excellence, leadership, and community service; [finalists receive travel assistance](https://nfb.org/programs-services/scholarships-and-awards/scholarship-program) to the national convention, where final awards are announced.

## Field-of-Study Scholarships

### Barry Goldwater Scholarship
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [Barry Goldwater Scholarship and Excellence in Education Foundation](https://fellowships.brown.edu/fellowships/goldwater-scholarship).
- **Award Amount:** Up to [7,500 United States dollars](https://onsa.asu.edu/scholarship/goldwater-scholarship) per academic year for up to two years, covering eligible undergraduate tuition, fees, books, and room and board.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** U.S. citizen, U.S. national, or permanent resident who is a full-time sophomore or junior in an accredited college, with [strong academic record](https://studentsuccess.utk.edu/urf/listings/goldwater/) and intent to pursue research careers in natural sciences, mathematics, or engineering.
- **Type of Scholarship:** [Field-based](https://fellowships.brown.edu/fellowships/goldwater-scholarship) (STEM research: natural sciences, mathematics, engineering).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Institutional nomination is required](https://studentsuccess.utk.edu/urf/listings/goldwater/); application includes research essay, transcripts, recommendations from research-active faculty, and short answers on research goals.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Highly competitive](https://onsa.asu.edu/scholarship/goldwater-scholarship), regarded as one of the most prestigious U.S. undergraduate STEM scholarships.
- **Key Notes:** Focuses on students with significant [undergraduate research experience](https://nationalscholarships.mines.edu/goldwaterscholarship/) and clear commitment to PhD-level research careers; each institution may nominate only a small number of students.

### SMART Scholarship-for-Service (Department of Defense)
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [U.S. Department of Defense (DoD)](https://www.dodstem.us/participate/smart/) through the SMART Scholarship-for-Service Program.
- **Award Amount:** [Full tuition and fees](https://scholarshipamerica.org/scholarship/smart-scholarship-program/), annual stipend (approximately 25,000–46,000 United States dollars depending on degree level), book and health allowances, paid summer internships, and [guaranteed civilian STEM employment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMART_Defense_Scholarship_Program) with DoD after graduation.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** U.S. citizen, minimum GPA around 3.0, enrolled or accepted in accredited undergraduate or graduate STEM degree programs, [able to obtain security clearance](https://www.nrl.navy.mil/Careers/Students/SMART-Scholarship-Program/), and willing to complete a service commitment at a DoD facility.
- **Type of Scholarship:** [Field-based (STEM)](https://scholarshipamerica.org/scholarship/smart-scholarship-program/), Affiliation-based (service obligation to DoD).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Online application](https://www.dodstem.us/participate/smart/) including transcripts, verification of citizenship, statements of interest, recommendations, and selection of sponsoring DoD facility.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Highly competitive](https://scholarshipamerica.org/scholarship/smart-scholarship-program/) due to generous funding and guaranteed employment.
- **Key Notes:** Scholarship duration typically [matched one-to-one](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMART_Defense_Scholarship_Program) with required years of post-graduation DoD service; covers undergraduate and graduate study in 20-plus STEM disciplines.

### NOAA Ernest F. Hollings Undergraduate Scholarship
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA)](https://scholarships.umd.edu/noaa-hollings).
- **Award Amount:** Up to [9,500 United States dollars](https://nationalscholarships.uconn.edu/hollings/) per academic year for two years of full-time undergraduate study plus a [10-week paid summer internship](https://curf.ku.edu/hollings-scholarship) at a NOAA facility with a stipend of about 700 United States dollars per week.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** [U.S. citizens](https://nationalscholarships.uconn.edu/hollings/) with minimum 3.0 GPA, typically rising juniors, majoring in fields related to [NOAA’s mission](https://drexel.edu/pennoni/urep/fellowships/finding-fellowships/noaa-hollings) such as oceanic, atmospheric, environmental, or related physical, biological, mathematical, social sciences, engineering, or STEM education.
- **Type of Scholarship:** [Field-based](https://drexel.edu/pennoni/urep/fellowships/finding-fellowships/noaa-hollings) (environmental, oceanic, atmospheric and related STEM fields).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Online application](https://scholarships.umd.edu/noaa-hollings), transcripts, recommendations, statement of interest describing alignment with NOAA’s mission, and documentation of major and GPA.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Highly competitive national program](https://nationalscholarships.uconn.edu/hollings/).
- **Key Notes:** Includes [structured internship and professional development](https://drexel.edu/pennoni/urep/fellowships/finding-fellowships/noaa-hollings); emphasizes preparation for public-service careers and education roles related to NOAA’s mission.

### NSF Scholarships in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (S-STEM)
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF)](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/s-stem-nsf-scholarships-science-technology-engineering-mathematics) via institutional S-STEM grants.
- **Award Amount:** Varies by institution; [S-STEM grants](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/s-stem-nsf-scholarships-science-technology-engineering-mathematics/nsf24-511) fund multiple scholarships and associated support activities, typically providing multi-thousand-dollar awards annually to academically talented, low-income STEM students.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** [U.S. students with financial need](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/s-stem-nsf-scholarships-science-technology-engineering-mathematics) and academic talent pursuing eligible STEM degrees (associate through doctoral) in disciplines where NSF funds research; specific GPA and income thresholds set by participating institutions.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Field-based (STEM), Circumstance-based (low-income/financial-need).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** Students apply through [participating colleges or universities](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10586752/), generally submitting financial aid information, academic records, and statements of interest; details vary by campus program.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** Competitive within each participating institution; availability limited to campuses with S-STEM awards.
- **Key Notes:** In addition to scholarships, programs typically include [mentoring, advising, research opportunities](https://pwvas.org/index.php/pwvas/article/view/1202), and community-building activities to improve retention and graduation in STEM.

## Affiliation-Based Scholarships

### Knights of Columbus U.S. Undergraduate Scholarships
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [Knights of Columbus Supreme Council](https://www.kofc.org/resources/scholarships/).
- **Award Amount:** Multiple annual [Fourth Degree Pro Deo and Pro Patria](https://www.kofc.org/resources/scholarships/us-undergraduate-scholarships/), John W. McDevitt, and endowed scholarships; individual scholarships commonly cover a portion of tuition for one academic year.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Entering first-year undergraduates at [approved Catholic colleges or universities](https://www.kofc.org/resources/scholarships/) in the United States, who are Knights of Columbus members in good standing or their eligible children; includes specific trusts for families of firefighters, law enforcement, or military.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Affiliation-based (membership or family relationship with Knights of Columbus; Catholic institution attendance).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Online application](https://www.kofc.org/resources/scholarships/us-undergraduate-scholarships/) within specified spring window, documentation of K of C membership or eligibility, academic records, and proof of enrollment at approved Catholic institution.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** Moderately competitive within the pool of eligible Knights’ families.
- **Key Notes:** Separate [state-level scholarships](https://kofcohio.org/programs/scholarships/) may exist, such as those offered by the Maryland State Council, with additional [local criteria](https://kofc-md.org/high-school-scholarships/).

### Elks National Foundation Most Valuable Student Scholarship
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [Elks National Foundation](https://www.elks.org/scholars/scholarships/mvs.cfm) (Benevolent and Protective Order of Elks).
- **Award Amount:** [Five hundred four-year scholarships](https://accessscholarships.com/scholarship/most-valuable-student-scholarship/) for high school seniors, with awards from 1,000 to 7,500 United States dollars per year; top 20 national finalists receive [30,000 United States dollars](https://www.scholarships.com/scholarships/elks-national-foundation-most-valuable-student-scholarship) each.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** [U.S. citizens](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/scholarships/elks-national-foundation-most-valuable-student-scholarship) who are high school seniors planning full-time bachelor’s study at accredited U.S. colleges; selection based on scholarship, leadership, and financial need; Elks lodge membership is not required.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Affiliation-based (fraternal organization sponsorship, but not limited to members), Circumstance-based (need-informed).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Online application](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/scholarships/elks-national-foundation-most-valuable-student-scholarship) including academic record, test scores, activities, and financial information; multi-stage competition from local lodge to national level, with top finalists invited to a [leadership weekend](https://www.elks.org/scholars/scholarships/mvs.cfm).
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** Highly competitive given national applicant pool and [limited top awards](https://www.scholarships.com/scholarships/elks-national-foundation-most-valuable-student-scholarship).
- **Key Notes:** Many [local lodges and state Elks associations](https://www.elks.org/scholars/scholarships/mvs.cfm) offer additional scholarships separate from the 500 national awards.

### National Eagle Scout Association (NESA) Scholarships
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [National Eagle Scout Association](https://nesa.org/scholarships/) (Boy Scouts of America).
- **Award Amount:** Multiple scholarship types, including [merit and need-based awards](https://nesa.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/NESA_Scholarship_Flyer_2025.pdf); notable awards include Lawrence S. and Mabel Cooke four-year scholarships up to about [48,000 United States dollars](https://www.scholarships.com/scholarships/national-eagle-scout-association-merit-scholarships) total.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** [Eagle Scouts who are NESA members](https://blog.scoutingmagazine.org/2024/11/11/nesa-scholarship-application-season-is-almost-here/), between senior year of high school and junior year of undergraduate study, enrolled or accepted full-time in accredited degree or skilled-trade programs.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Affiliation-based (Eagle Scout rank and NESA membership).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Online application during a defined winter window](https://www.ghvscouting.org/blog/2025/11/18/2026nesascholarships/), including academic records, activities, community service, Scouting involvement, recommendations, and essays.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Highly competitive](https://blog.scoutingmagazine.org/2024/11/11/nesa-scholarship-application-season-is-almost-here/) within the Eagle Scout population.
- **Key Notes:** Scholarships recognize leadership and service in Scouting; recipients may only [receive NESA scholarship funding once](https://nesa.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/NESA_Scholarship_Flyer_2025.pdf).

### Coca-Cola First Generation Scholarship Programs (Institution-Partnered)
- **Sponsoring Organizations:** [The Coca-Cola Foundation](https://www.uh.edu/cse/scholarships/coca-cola/index.php) in partnership with individual universities and with [UNCF](https://nccu.academicworks.com/opportunities/2972) and [HACU](https://hacu.net/hacu-is-accepting-applications-for-the-coca-cola-first-generation-scholarships-2/) for selected programs.
- **Award Amount:** Commonly around [5,000 United States dollars](https://studentscholarships.org/scholarship/28348/coca-cola-first-generation-scholarship-program) per year or per awardee; some [campus programs](https://www.uh.edu/cse/scholarships/coca-cola/index.php) provide 5,000 United States dollars total or per year to cohorts of first-generation students.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** [First-generation college students](https://nccu.academicworks.com/opportunities/2972) (parents without four-year degrees) meeting institutional criteria, typically demonstrating financial need and enrolling full time at participating institutions.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Affiliation-based (attendance at participating institutions or member campuses), Circumstance-based (first-generation, financial need).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** Applications generally [routed through institutional scholarship portals](https://hacu.net/hacu-is-accepting-applications-for-the-coca-cola-first-generation-scholarships-2/) or UNCF/HACU systems; typically require FAFSA, essays, transcripts, and confirmation of first-generation status.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** Moderately to highly competitive within each campus or national partner program.
- **Key Notes:** Some programs pair monetary awards with [cohort-based advising and student-success programming](https://campus.kennesaw.edu/faculty-staff/academic-affairs/student-success/programs/coca-cola-scholars/index.php) for first-gen and, in some cases, STEM students.

## Interest or Hobby Based Scholarships

### Duck Brand Stuck at Prom Scholarship Contest
- **Sponsoring Organization:** Duck Brand (Shurtape Technologies, LLC).
- **Award Amount:** [Grand prize scholarships of 10,000 United States dollars](https://www.fastweb.com/student-news/articles/stuck-at-prom-scholarship-contest) each for best dress and best tux categories; runners-up typically receive 1,000 United States dollars and product prize packs.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** High school and homeschool students in the United States and Canada who [create and wear prom attire](https://www.duckbrand.com/stuck-at-prom) made primarily from Duck Brand duct tape; must meet age and residency [requirements](https://www.duckbrand.com/stuck-at-prom/faqs) specified in each year’s rules.
- **Type of Scholarship:** [Interest-based](https://www.duckbrand.com/stuck-at-prom) (design, fashion, DIY craft contest with unusual materials).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** Submission of [high-resolution photos](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/duck-brand-celebrates-national-promposal-day-with-2026-stuck-at-prom-scholarship-contest-announcement-302711099.html) of the duct-tape prom outfit, along with an online entry form; public voting and panel judging are used to select winners.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Highly competitive](https://www.duckbrand.com/stuck-at-prom), especially for grand prizes, but niche due to the significant creative effort required.
- **Key Notes:** Contest does not require actually attending prom; judging criteria emphasize [workmanship, originality, and color use](https://www.duckbrand.com/stuck-at-prom/faqs).

### Vegetarian Resource Group Scholarship
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [The Vegetarian Resource Group (VRG)](https://vrg.org/student/scholar.htm).
- **Award Amount:** One scholarship of 10,000 United States dollars plus [multiple scholarships of 5,000 United States dollars](https://accessscholarships.com/scholarship/the-vegetarian-resource-group-scholarship/) (for example, one 10,000 United States dollars award and five 5,000 United States dollars awards in a recent cycle).
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Graduating U.S. high school seniors who are [vegetarians or vegans](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/scholarships/vegetarian-resource-group-scholarship) and who have promoted vegetarianism or veganism in their schools or communities.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Interest-based (ethical/dietary lifestyle: vegetarianism/veganism), Circumstance-based (sustained advocacy work).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Application form](https://vrg.org/student/scholar.htm) or equivalent typed submission, transcripts, essay describing promotion of vegetarianism or veganism, evidence of advocacy and leadership, and recommendations.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** Niche and [competitive national award](https://accessscholarships.com/scholarship/the-vegetarian-resource-group-scholarship/) focused on a narrow advocacy area.
- **Key Notes:** Awards are [paid directly to U.S. colleges](https://vrg.org/student/scholar.htm); applicants are assessed on compassion, courage, and commitment to promoting a peaceful world through vegetarian or vegan lifestyle.

### Tall Clubs International Foundation Scholarship
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [Tall Clubs International Foundation, Inc.](https://tallclubfoundation.org/scholarship/scholarship/)
- **Award Amount:** [Annual scholarships](https://tall.org/home/tci-gives-back/tci-foundation/) of approximately 1,000 United States dollars each to multiple high school seniors across North America.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** High school seniors meeting [minimum height requirements](https://accessscholarships.com/scholarship/tall-clubs-international-foundation-scholarship/)—at least 5 feet 10 inches for women and 6 feet 2 inches for men—planning to begin full-time study at accredited institutions.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Demographic/interest-based (physical characteristic: tall stature).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** Applicants typically [request sponsorship](https://tallclubfoundation.org/scholarship/scholarship/) from a local Tall Club or member-at-large, then submit height verification, academic records, and nomination materials.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Niche and limited pool](https://www.scholarships.com/scholarships/tall-clubs-international-student-scholarships) due to strict height requirements and the specific nomination process.
- **Key Notes:** Many [local Tall Clubs](https://tall.org/home/tci-gives-back/tci-foundation/) offer their own individual scholarships, from which nominees are forwarded to the national foundation awards.

### Davis-Putter Scholarship Fund (Student Activism)
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [Davis-Putter Scholarship Fund](https://www.davisputter.org).
- **Award Amount:** Need-based grants [up to 15,000 United States dollars](https://grad.uchicago.edu/fellowship/davis-putter-scholarship-fund/) per year, with average awards around 7,500 United States dollars in recent cycles.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Students enrolled in college, university, trade, or technical programs who are [actively engaged in progressive movements](https://accessscholarships.com/scholarship/davis-putter-scholarship-fund/) for social and economic justice and who demonstrate financial need.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Interest-based (social-justice activism and organizing), Circumstance-based (financial need).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** Online application [due by April 1](https://justiceineducation.columbia.edu/opportunity-davis-putter-scholarships-for-students-working-for-social-change/) including transcripts, financial information, detailed statements about activist work, and recommendations.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Highly competitive](https://grad.uchicago.edu/fellowship/davis-putter-scholarship-fund/) among nationally active student organizers.
- **Key Notes:** Emphasizes [long-term commitment to social change](https://www.davisputter.org) rather than purely academic metrics; open to both U.S. and non-U.S. citizens involved in U.S.-based movements.

## Circumstance-Based Scholarships

### Patsy Takemoto Mink Education Support Award
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [Patsy Takemoto Mink Education Foundation](https://www.patsyminkfoundation.org).
- **Award Amount:** Education Support Awards of [up to 5,000 United States dollars](https://www.scholarships.com/scholarships/patsy-takemoto-mink-foundation-scholarship) each, typically disbursed in two installments over an academic year.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Women at least 17 years old who are [mothers with minor children](https://www.patsyminkfoundation.org), from low-income families, pursuing first degrees or credentials at non-profit accredited U.S. institutions.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Circumstance-based (low-income mothers), Demographic (gender identity).
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Application](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/scholarships/patsy-takemoto-mink-education-foundation-education-support-scholarship) including personal and educational information, essays about circumstances and goals, income documentation, and references; deadlines typically in late summer.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** Highly competitive, with a limited number of national awards.
- **Key Notes:** Funds may be used for both [direct school costs and living expenses](https://www.buffalo.edu/fellowships/funding/mink.html) while enrolled; the foundation centers educational access and equity for low-income women.

### Jeannette Rankin National Scholar Grant

  - **Sponsoring Organization:** [Jeannette Rankin Foundation](https://rankinfoundation.org).
  - **Award Amount:** Scholar Grants [up to 2,500 United States dollars](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://rankinfoundation.org/for-students/) annually, renewable for up to five years and disbursed directly to recipients as non-tuition support.
  - **Eligibility Criteria:** [Women and nonbinary students age 35 or older](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://accessscholarships.com/scholarship/jeannette-rankin-women-scholarship-fund/) who are U.S. citizens or permanent residents, demonstrate financial need, and are pursuing technical or vocational education, associate’s degrees, or first bachelor’s degrees.
  - **Type of Scholarship:** Circumstance-based (adult learners with financial need), Demographic (women and nonbinary people).
  - **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Online application](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://rankinfoundation.org/for-students/) including essay responses, income and enrollment documentation, recommendations, and transcripts; an eligibility survey and checklist guide applicants.
  - **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Competitive](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://rankinfoundation.org/our-impact/), with a limited number of grants relative to the high volume of adult learner applicants.
  - **Key Notes:** Grants are [unrestricted non-tuition funds](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://rankinfoundation.org/for-students/), allowing recipients to cover living expenses; the five-year renewability is designed specifically to support the longer timelines of part-time and returning students.

### Foster Care to Success (FC2S) Scholarships

  - **Sponsoring Organization:** [Foster Care to Success](https://www.fc2success.org/our-programs/information-for-students/).
  - **Award Amount:** Scholarships generally ranging from [1,500 to 6,000 United States dollars](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.scholarships.com/scholarships/foster-care-to-success-scholarships) per academic year, renewable for up to five years based on satisfactory progress.
  - **Eligibility Criteria:** Young people [under age 25](https://www.fc2success.org/our-programs/information-for-students/) who spent at least 12 consecutive months in U.S. foster care leading up to their 18th birthday, or were adopted/placed into guardianship after age 16; must be enrolled in accredited [Pell-eligible programs](https://www.fc2success.org/our-programs/information-for-students/).
  - **Type of Scholarship:** Circumstance-based (foster care, adoption from care, or orphan status).
  - **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Online application](https://www.fc2success.org/our-programs/information-for-students/), documentation of foster-care status, transcripts, and personal statements; the application window typically closes [annually in late March or early April](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.scholarships.com/scholarships/foster-care-to-success-scholarships).
  - **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** Competitive within the population of eligible current and former foster youth.
  - **Key Notes:** FC2S also manages [specifically sponsored scholarships](https://www.fc2success.org/our-programs/information-for-students/) with additional criteria such as specific majors or geographic locations; the organization provides [mentoring and care packages](https://www.fc2success.org/our-programs/information-for-students/) in addition to financial aid.

### Cancer for College Scholarships

  - **Sponsoring Organization:** [Cancer for College](https://cancerforcollege.org).
  - **Award Amount:** One-time and recurring scholarships, often around [5,000 United States dollars](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://accessscholarships.com/scholarship/cancer-for-college-scholarship/) per year; the organization has awarded more than [2,000 scholarships](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://cancerforcollege.org/scholarships/) totaling about 8 million United States dollars to survivors.
  - **Eligibility Criteria:** Current or [former cancer patients or survivors](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://cancerforcollege.org/scholarships/) who are U.S. citizens and planning to enroll at accredited four-year universities, community colleges, or graduate schools; some awards are [region-specific](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://cancerforcollege.org/scholarships/) (e.g., California or the Pacific Northwest).
  - **Type of Scholarship:** Circumstance-based (serious illness: cancer survivorship).
  - **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Online application](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://cancerforcollege.org/scholarships/) during an annual window (typically October through January 31st), documentation of cancer diagnosis, transcripts, financial information, and essays.
  - **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Highly competitive](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://cancerforcollege.org/scholarships/) among cancer survivors nationwide.
  - **Key Notes:** Beyond tuition, the organization offers [mentorship, medical debt relief, and networking](https://cancerforcollege.org) opportunities; it was founded by a two-time cancer survivor to help others overcome the financial burden of the disease.

### Horatio Alger Association Undergraduate Scholarships
- **Sponsoring Organization:** [Horatio Alger Association of Distinguished Americans](https://horatioalger.org).
- **Award Amount:** Multiple programs including [National Scholarships of up to 25,000 United States dollars](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/scholarships/horatio-alger-association-national--state-scholarship) and state or targeted scholarships often around 10,000 United States dollars; the association awards over 1,200 scholarships annually.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Students with [significant financial need](https://www.scholarships.com/scholarships/horatio-alger-undergraduate-scholarship) (typically an adjusted gross family income of 65,000 United States dollars or lower) who have faced and overcome substantial adversity, maintain at least a 2.0 GPA, and plan to pursue bachelor’s degrees; must be U.S. citizens.
- **Type of Scholarship:** Circumstance-based (financial need and documented adversity), sometimes targeted by state or career path.
- **Application Requirements (Brief):** [Single online application](https://horatioalger.org/scholarships-2/) for many programs including detailed adversity essays, [Support Form](https://horatioalger.org/counselor-and-provider-faq/) from a non-relative adult over 21, Counselor Certification Form, high school transcripts, and income verification.
- **Competitiveness Level (Estimated):** [Highly competitive](https://horatioalger.org/graduate-scholarships/) national program that prioritizes character, perseverance, and determination over purely academic metrics.
- **Key Notes:** Beyond financial aid, the program provides comprehensive [Scholar Services](https://scholars.horatioalger.org/scholar-services/) including mental health support, emergency financial assistance, college success tools, and a 24/7 support line; notable for [identifying and supporting students](https://www.cms.mntm.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Horatio-Alger.pdf) as early as their high school junior year.
